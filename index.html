Project Overview

Background

RevoCommerce runs a high-volume, multi-channel B2C and B2B model on Amazon India. Q2 2022 revenue totaled ₹70.97M, but monthly revenue fell 20% from April to June. The analysis evaluated 125,000+ transactions to find the operational bottlenecks behind the drop, with a goal of increasing profitability by 25% in 30 days.

Method

  • Data Cleaning: Imputed 2,951 missing Amount values from average SKU pricing and removed 3,206 outliers using the IQR method.
  • Segmentation: Segmented orders by fulfillment channel (Amazon FBA vs Merchant) and tracked the lifecycle of 6,474 'Unshipped' items.
  • Analysis: Mapped revenue and units by category, and correlated daily Kurta AOV with store revenue. Built a Random Forest model to confirm the key drivers of failed orders.

Result

  • Fulfillment Gap: Amazon FBA successfully completed 86.72% of orders vs 75.65% for Merchant operations. Merchant fulfillment carried a 17.67% cancellation rate.
  • Unshipped Trap: 84.4% of 'Unshipped' orders ended up cancelled, resulting in about 5,462 lost transactions in Q2.
  • Predictive Model: The Random Forest model proved Merchant fulfillment was the #1 predictor of cancellations (38.6% weight, 87% recall).
  • Actionable Recommendation: Enforced a 24-hour dispatch SLA with a 12-hour alert, migrated high-AOV categories to FBA, and stabilized Kurta pricing as a loss leader, estimating an impact of +₹6.65M (+31.52% projected uplift).

Project Overview

Background

RevoBank generated Rp 456.88B in credit card sales volume over the past 6 months, yielding Rp 6.85B in gross MDR fees and Rp 5.84B in net profit after absorbing fraud losses. To support the Performance Management team in increasing transaction frequency and card utilization, this analysis audits 6-month sales performance and builds unsupervised customer personas across credit risk and financial capacity.

Method

  • Data Cleaning: Stripped formatting anomalies from 5,599 card records, fixed brand casing, purged 31 duplicate IDs, and excluded 40 invalid/expired cards.
  • Feature Engineering: Imputed null 6-month transaction counts and engineered User Age, a boolean Retired Flag, and Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratio features.
  • Modeling: Selected 4-Cluster K-Means Segmentation over traditional RFM because traditional RFM only measures past transactions and misclassifies wealthy, debt-free dormant users as "lost."

Result

  • Demographic Risk Gap: 'Very Good' credit users drive peak volume at low DTI (0.246), while the 'Poor' credit tier spends anomalously high amounts despite a 0.302 DTI ratio.
  • Cluster Personas: Identified Cluster 1 (Dormant Earners) as holding the highest income (102.3K) and low DTI (0.13), but generating 0.00% transaction volume.
  • Actionable Recommendation: Launched luxury cashback "Top-of-Wallet" activation for Cluster 1, deployed frequency points for Clusters 0 & 2, and recommended freezing automatic limit increases for Cluster 3 & 'Poor' credit tiers.

Project Overview

Background

Faced with unclear metrics on which service lines and doctor experience tiers generate the highest net revenue and yield, Revo Hospital risked allocating budget and bed capacity to high-volume, low-margin departments. The goal was to pinpoint high-yield medical service lines and direct strategic department budgeting.

Method

  • Data Architecture: Built a central admissions fact table with a 1-to-1 billing connection to ensure financial data integrity and mitigate Cartesian product duplication risks.
  • Calculations: Modeled admissions, billing, department, and doctor data, incorporating Level of Detail (LOD) expressions for department yield and YTD running totals.
  • Visualization: Designed a strategic executive dashboard in Tableau specifically tailored for the Finance Lead to monitor real-time revenue breakdowns and doctor experience tiers.

Result

  • Yield Identification: Successfully pinpointed Oncology (Rp46.9M per admission) and Cardiology (Rp28.8M per admission) as the highest-yield departments.
  • Utilization Gap: Data revealed that mid-tier doctors (6-10 years of experience) were significantly underutilized compared to patient volume loads.
  • Actionable Recommendation: Recommended prioritizing high-yield bed capacity for Oncology and routing more non-critical cases to the under-utilized mid-tier doctors to optimize both revenue and patient care bandwidth.
